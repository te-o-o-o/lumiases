# Changelog

## 0.1.0 (2026-10-28)

### Added

- A living orb in a terminal pane, drawn in braille or ASCII, plus eight
  other looks (wedge, shade, bars, halftone, contour, engrave, matrix,
  geometric); `s` steps through them.
- Eight states (idle, thinking, reading, editing, running, waiting, error,
  done), each with its own shape, speed and colours, morphing into the next.
- Eyes in five shapes, with an expression per state.
- Nine scenes drawn from code alone besides the orb: a swarm, pulsar ridge
  lines, an oscilloscope, field lines, a contour map, waves, a spectrum,
  orbits and a flow field; `h` steps through them.
- `lumiases hook` and `-follow`: the pane follows a Claude Code session
  through its hooks.
- Statue mode: the matter flows over a photo of a statue, keyed out of a
  plain background when it has no transparency; `+` and `-` zoom.
- A GIF intro played at start in Ghostty, kitty and WezTerm, with a contrast
  or digital filter.
